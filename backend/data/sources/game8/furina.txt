Tier Rating
Furina solidifies herself as an all-around buffer that surpasses many other options due to her augmenting every form of damage that allies can do, giving her plenty of options and versatility as to where she can slot herself into. She also gains more value as a Sub-DPS, as both her buffs and her damage scale with HP, so the stronger her buffs are, the stronger her personal damage becomes. These numbers reach incredibly respective heights, and the damage she can bring to the table potentially surpasses the other buffer units without sacrificing her supportive capabilities.

When selecting buffer units for a team, Furina, Bennett, Kazuha, and now Xilonen are often the premier choices due to their ease of use and the varied effectiveness of her buffs. Furina's main drawback is that her buffs need to be activated by having a competent healer on the team that can stack her fanfare points. While this is inherently a downside, most team compositions are able to accommodate both Furina and a healer onto their roster, with Xilonen and Bennett being completely synergistic with her. This puts her in a better spot as opposed to being potentially overtaken by other buffers due to how well most team comps complement Furina and how much Furina can offer to her teammates.

Furina Strengths and Weaknesses
Strengths
• Good damage scaling for her Ousia form, and her Elemental Skill can be refreshed indefinitely.
• Great universal buffer for damage with her Fanfare mechanic as long as you have a healer.
• HP drain to her party members opens up unique playstyles involving HP fluctuations. Certain characters can now equip HP-change dependent artifacts and weapons, as long as you have Furina in your team.
• Her being able to heal off-field with her Pneuma form, and her ability to walk on water with her Elemental Skill active, are highly convenient perks for overworld exploration.
Weaknesses
• Teams with Furina require a strong party healer to help her gain Fanfare stacks as well as mitigate the HP loss dealt by her Ousia summons.
• Switching her Arkhe removes the current summon from the field, so her team comps depend on the playstyle and build she's currently using, and therefore isn't flexible.
• Most new released units don't need her DMG% buff anymore.
Furina Best Builds
Furina Sub-DPS and Support Build
When building Furina, take note that although most of her buffs scale with HP, she also does considerable damage when given proper CRIT stats and a Hydro DMG Bonus goblet.

Furina's buffs cap out at the 40,000 HP mark, so try your best to reach as close to that breakpoint as realistically possible, while also accounting for stats such as Energy Recharge and CRIT if using her as a proper Sub-DPS.

Furina is mainly built around HP since most of her buffs and damage scale off of that stat, regardless if you want her to be primarily support or sub-dps. This build pattern is dictated by her A4 passive, which increases the damage of her summons according to her HP value.

In general, you would want to have an HP/HP for Sands and Goblet and then having either CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG for her Circlet, depending on which stat you need prioritize for a balance ratio.

Furina Goal Stat Values
Stat	Goal Value
HP	35,000 ~ 40,000 HP
CRIT Rate	60 ~ 80%
CRIT DMG	160 ~ 200% or above
Energy Recharge	180% or above
As mentioned from above, you would want to have as much HP as you can with Furina. The 40,000 HP buff cap is based on her A4 passive. It is important to have Furina around 35,000 ~ 40,000 at all times to fully capitalize on her potential.

Energy Recharge needs are dependent on how many other Hydro units are in the team with Furina, but a good baseline is around ~180% to ensure that she can use her Elemental Burst every rotation.

Other than that, your CRIT value should have a 2:1 ratio for perfect balance.

Furina Talent Priority
1st	2nd	3rd
Elemental Burst	Elemental Skill	Normal Attack
Furina's Elemental Skill and Elemental Burst are equally important. However, Elemental Burst gets an edge because of the massive team buff that it offers. Despite that, you should level both equally.

Normal Attack can be mostly ignored. Even at C6, her damage scales to her HP, not Talent levels.

Furina Best Weapons
Furina Uses HP or ER Weapons
When it comes to weapon choice, if not using Furina's Splendor of Tranquil Waters, then other HP or Energy Recharge weapons such as the Key of Khaj-Nisut or Festering Desire as the primary go-tos.

These are usually meant to even out Furina's stats, as she needs as much HP as she can get while also having a healthy amount of Energy Recharge and potentially CRIT.

Furina Best Team Comps
Best as a Buffer in Teams
Furina's teams usually make use of her as a Sub-DPS and Buffer for units that appreciates DMG% buff from her Elemental Burst. Her Elemental Skill allows consistent off-field hydro application that can provide a lot of benefit for units that need Hydro aura.

She needs to be paired with Healers to properly generate fanfare from her Burst.

Furina Talents and How to Use
Normal Attack
Normal Attack: Soloist's Solicitation
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.
Charge Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash a solo dance, dealing Physical DMG to nearby opponents and changing her Arkhe alignment. If Salon Members or Singer of Many Waters summoned by her Elemental Skill "Salon Solitaire" are present, their lineup will switch in response.

Arkhe: Seats Sacred and Secular
At intervals, when Furina's Normal Attacks hit, a Spiritbreath Thorn or a Surging Blade will descend based on her current alignment, dealing Hydro DMG based on her current alignment.
When Furina takes the field, her starting Arkhe will be Ousia.
Skill Attributes
Changing Arkhe Alignments
Furina can change her Arkhe alignment by using her Charged Attacks, which will also directly affect how her other attacks behave. Ousia Furina leans on for off-field DMG, while Pneuma is for healing.

Pneuma and Ousia Explained (Arkhe System)

Elemental Skill
Salon Solitaire
Invites the guests of the Salon Solitaire to come forth and abet in Furina's performance. Will summon either the Salon Members or the Singer of Many Waters based on Furina's current Arkhe alignment.
Ousia
Foaming bubbles like celebrants shall dance, dealing AoE Hydro DMG based on Furina's Max HP and summoning 3 Salon Members: the Ball Octopus-shaped Gentilhomme Usher, the Bubbly Seahorse-shaped Surintendante Chevalmarin, and the Armored Crab-shaped Mademoiselle Crabaletta.
They will attack nearby opponents at intervals, prioritizing the target of the active character, dealing Hydro DMG based on Max HP.
When they attack, if character(s) with more than 50% HP are nearby, the Members will increase their current attack's power based on the number of such characters, and consume said characters' HP. If the characters who meet these requirements are 1/2/3/4 (or more), the Members' attacks will deal 110%/120%/130%/140% of their original DMG. Pneuma
Summons the Singer of Many Waters, who will heal nearby active character(s) based on Max HP at intervals.
The Salon Members and Singer of Many Waters share a duration, and when Furina uses her Charged Attack to change the guest type, the new guests will inherit the initial duration. While the Salon Members and the Singer of Many Waters are on the field, Furina can move on the water's surface."
Skill Attributes
Damage Dealing or Healing Summon
Easily her best asset, Furina's Elemental Skill summons her minions that either deals DMG to opponents (Ousia) or heals party members (Pneuma) based on her Arkhe alignment!

Ousia Summons
Furina summons Salon Members that will deal DMG to surrounding opponents, prioritizing your active character's target.

The Salon Members consume your team's HP at regular intervals during their attacks on a cap. Consuming your character's HP buffs the troupe's damage based on how many characters the members absorb HP from!

Pneuma Summons
Furina calls forth the Singer of Many Waters, which will heal nearby active members!

Can Walk on Water While Skill is Active
While the Summons are active, Furina gains the ability to walk on water!

Elemental Burst
Let the People Rejoice
Rouses the impulse to revel, creating a stage of foam that will deal AoE Hydro DMG based on Furina's Max HP and cause nearby party members to enter the Universal Revelry state:
During this time, when nearby party members' HP increases or decreases, 1 Fanfare point will be granted to Furina for each percentage point of their Max HP by which their HP changes.
At the same time, Furina will increase the DMG dealt by and Incoming Healing Bonus of all nearby party members based on the amount of Fanfare she has. When the duration ends, Furina's Fanfare points will be cleared.
Skill Attributes
Massive Buffs Based on HP Fluctuations
Deals Hydro DMG to surrounding opponents, and gives buffs when HP fluctuations are triggered. When the party members' HP increases or decreases, Furina gains Fanfare stacks, which buffs all their DMG and incoming healing bonus!
