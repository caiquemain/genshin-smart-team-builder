Neuvillette is a 5-star Hydro character that wields a Catalyst in Genshin Impact. He functions as an on-field, main DPS that utilizes his HP to scale powerful Charged Attacks to release torrents of water on unsuspecting enemies. Additionally, Neuvillette can generate "Sourcewater Droplets" to further enhance his powerful Charged Attacks at the cost of his HP. Lore-wise, Neuvillette is the Iudex of Fontaine, who's main purpose is to arbitrate, or to make the final judgements during a case. Despite his human appearance, Neuvillette is actually the Hydro Dragon and one of the Seven Sovereigns.

Roles & Playstyle of Neuvillette and His Strengths and Weakness
Neuvillette is meant to be played as a DPS. His ability to easily dish out damage without being too reliant on Energy Recharge makes him easier to use compared to other DPS characters.

Pros and Cons of DPS Neuvillette
Pros
- Easy to use and has flexible team comp options.
- Great at both single target and multiple enemies situation.
Cons
- Prone to interruptions without his Constellation 1 but a shielder can solve that problem easily.
- HP mechanic can be difficult to get used to but a shielder or sustain support can help ease the difficulty.

Basic Team Rotation for DPS Neuvillette
Basic Rotation For DPS Neuvillette
1. Use Sub-DPS and Support characters abilities.
2. Switch to Neuvillette and use his Elemental Burst then Skill.
3. Hold Attack button to continuously deal damage.
4. Repeat Step 3 until Skill or Burst goes off cooldown or Sourcewater Droplets run out.
Neuvillette's gameplay as a DPS is to use the off-field character abilities then generate Sourcewater Droplets before he spams Charged Attacks for damage. You can choose to generate the Droplets first to apply Hydro for VV supports or Xilonen then resume off-field character rotation.

Basics of Using Neuvillette (Beginner Guide)
HP-Scaling Hydro DPS with HP Mechanics
Neuvillette is a DPS character who primarily deals damage with his Charged Attacks that can drain his HP. His HP drain mechanic is easily alleviated by the healing he gets from absorbing Sourcewater Droplets he generates.

Can Use Marechaussee Hunter Set
Summary of Marechaussee Hunter Effects
2-pc Set Effect:
- Increases Normal Attack and Charged Attack DMG by 15%.
4-pc Set Effect:
- Buffs CRIT Rate by 12% (stacks up to 3) for 5s when HP increases or decreases.
Neuvillette is one of the few DPS characters who is able to decrease his own HP, making him able to use Marechaussee Hunter without needing Furina to drain HP. Furina is still a good support for Neuvillette since his HP drain allows her to stack faster for her DMG buff.

Needs Sourcewater Droplets for Charged Attack Spam
Normally, Neuvillette's Charged Attack takes a while to charge. Neuvillette can speed up the charging phase by absorbing nearby Sourcewater Droplets. These Droplets can be created by Neuvillette himself, Hydro Traveler and Sigewinne.

Both Elemental Skill and Elemental Burst Creates Sourcewater Droplets
Neuvillette is able to create Sourcewater Droplets using his Elemental Skill and Burst. His Skill gives 3 Sourcewater Droplets while his Burst gives 6. Neuvillette only needs 3 Sourcewater Droplets to instantly use his Charged Attack so it's best to use his Burst then Skill so you can use at least 3 Charged Attacks before needing to refresh them.

At Least 2 Unique Hydro Reactions Recommended
Neuvillette's Passive allows him to obtain up to 3 stacks that can boost his Charged Attack DMG. These stacks can be obtained by triggering all Hydro reactions aside from Vaporize. If Furina is on the team, you can use 2 Hydro Reactions since her damage buff can make up for the 3rd.

How to Use Neuvillette?
How To Use?
▼Top Tier Charged Attack Hydro DPS
▼Use Hydro Reactions to Increase His Damage
▼Use E. Skill and E. Burst to Create Droplets
▼Use Charged Attack to Absorb Droplets and Deal Damage
▼Charged Attack can be Used For Floating Over Water
Top Tier Charged Attack Hydro DPS
Neuvillette is the current best Hydro DPS. This is due to his damage scaling and ease of use. Building a team for Neuvillette only has the requirement of being able to enable Hydro-related reactions so that his passive effect will be triggered and he will be able to obtain stacks. With how long lasting these stacks are, only a single trigger of such reactions from every member is needed for his rotations.

Things Neuvillette Can Do
★ Deal Powerful Hydro damage with his Charged Attacks
★ Makes use of Hydro-related reactions to increase Charged Attack damage
★ Create Sourcewater Droplets with his E. Skill and E. Burst
★ Absorb existing Sourcewater Droplets to reduce charging time for his Charged Attacks
★ Charged Attack' charging period can be used to float over bodies of water
Use Hydro Reactions to Increase His Damage
Neuvillette
The passive effect of Neuvillette allows him to gain stacks each time a Hydro-related reaction happens. Each stack will increase the damage dealt by his Charged Attacks. With this in mind, there is a lot of potential teammates for Neuvillette since he does not strictly need the Vaporize reaction to increase his damage greatly.

Use E. Skill and E. Burst to Create Droplets
Both his E. Skill and E. Burst can create Sourcewater Droplets for Neuvillette to use. Aside from Neuvillette, there are only two characters that can create Sourcewater Droplets, the Hydro Traveler and Sigewinne. Though both do not have any particular synergy with his kit, making them both relatively uncommon in his team comps.

Use Charged Attack to Absorb Droplets and Deal Damage
Sourcewater Droplets can drastically shorten the charging period of his Charged Attack. Without it, he will take a good few seconds charging and shortening the golden period of dealing damage with the team buffs. His Charged Attack also decreases his health but the Sourcewater Droplet absorption heals it back

Charged Attack is Good For Floating Over Water
It may seem like a waste to use the Charged Attack without Sourcewater Droplets but it has a great purpose for exploration! The charging period and the actual attack duration makes Neuvillette float for some time which can be used to cross bodies of water.

Should You Pull for Neuvillette's Constellation Or Best Weapon?
C1 is a High Value Constellation
Signature VS Constellations
C1 or Tome of the Eternal FlowTome of the Eternal Flow > C2
C1 Improves Various Team Performances
Neuvillette's C1 enables you to run him in teams that would not give him all three stacks from his "Heir to the Ancient Sea's Authority" passive, with the added interruption resistance also allowing you to refrain from totally needing a shield character. All of this makes it so that Neuvillete gains full benefit from this passive in teams where he normally wouldn't, which would elevate the total party damage overall.

However, while impactful, it is not necessary, and you'll find that Neuvillette will still be a reliable DPS character even without constellations. Getting his signature weapon is still a viable choice, especially if you have no plans to obtain the Battlepass Catalyst, Sacrificial Jade.

Recommendation if You Have Sacrificial Jade
C1 > C2 > Tome of the Eternal FlowTome of the Eternal Flow
High Refines on Sacrificial Jade is Competitive
R5 (max refinement) Sacrificial Jade is almost as good as his signature weapon. R1 (no refinement) Sacrificial Jade is a decent alternative still, but it will be outclassed by Tome of the Eternal Flow. In this case, you can opt into getting investing in his constellations, or look to get his signature weapon still if you have no plans to max refine the Sacrificial Jade.

Who Is Neuvillette?
The Iudex of Fontaine
neuvillette iudex
Neuvillette is the Iudex of Fontaine. He represents the supreme justice in the nation. With the Oratice, Neuvillette aims to judge in a just and fair manner.

Leader of the Marechaussee Phantom
Neuvillette also leads the Marechaussee Phantom, an offshoot organization of the Marechaussee Hunters. Marechaussee Phantom is a special detective force in Fontaine, consisting primarily of Melusines.

The Hydro Dragon Sovereign
neuvillette sovereign
During the Fontaine Archon Quest storyline, it was revealed that Neuvillette was the incarnation of the Hydro Dragon Sovereign. It was not until the end of the Archon Quest storyline, that he regained the full Authority of Hydro.

A Water Connoisseur
Perhaps in relation to his identity as the Hydro Sovereign, Neuvillette enjoys water tasting. In his voicelines, he has a long dialogue talking about the flavor profile of waters in different nations. In the 4.4 Lantern Rite event, Neuvillette visited the Qiaoying village and tasted the local spring water.
