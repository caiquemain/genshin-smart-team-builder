7.2 Phase 1 Post-Release Changes (November 04, 2026)
Name	Tier Updates
Genshin - Mitya C0Mitya
Genshin - SS Main DPS NEW
Mitya is the current culmination of three patches worth of development onto Stellar-Conduct as a team archetype and an Elemental reaction. It sees the convenience of having the Polestar Field be a mobile aura that follows the active character, and built that directly into Mitya's kit, making him one of the more convenient characters to have when it comes to using Stellar-Conduct.

In terms of Mitya himself, he performs a dual role in his teams as either the Main DPS, utilizing his Gradient Beacons as damage bonuses to his Charged Attack, or as a Support, where-in he spawns his Beacons as Off-Field application for his respective DPS. This dual role gives him plenty of space to slot into most Stellar-Conduct variations, but it's more of a weighted scale, as his best-in-slot role is that of a Main DPS. To that end, he performs this role exceptionally well, with a simple but ridiculously effective playstyle.

One of the only things that Mitya has stacked against him is that Stellar-Conduct is inherently more Cryo-focused than Electro-focused when it comes to On-Field DPS units. There is an awkward feeling of knowing that your only real Cryo support for Mitya as of right now is Odette, and she is pretty much shared across all Stellar teams as of now. This gap may widen further when the Tsaritsa and Danica roll around, as they will make this "Cryo over Electro" hierarchy even more pronounced, but only time will tell. For now, Mitya appears to be the best Electro-based Stellar-Conduct DPS, and will likely continue to be the king of that niche for a long while.
Genshin - Valeriy C6Valeriy	
Genshin - S SupportNEW
Valeriy is an interesting case as a Shielder-Buffer hybrid that scales entirely off ATK, and provides buffs exclusively towards Electro characters. Judging from how his kit works, it's clear that he was made to support Mitya and specifically Mitya due to the latter's reliance on interruption resistance and the former providing Electro-specific boons.

Apart from that, he functions as a standard Electro shielder and buffer, which gives him a spot in teams where the Main DPS is an Electro unit. This lets him slot into Overload, Stellar-Conduct, Lunar-Charged, and even Aggravate teams as a Shielder.

Due to this niche, however, this is both his calling and his shackle since he is not as effective as a general Electro unit, and he ends up dropping in value when not paired with a Stellar ally like Mitya or Cyno.
Genshin - Alyosha C6Alyosha	
Genshin - S Tier Up Support
With Stellar-Conduct having enough time to grow, Alyosha has seen more use due to the robustness of his Electro application when it comes to Stellar-Conduct teams. This gives him a small raise in rank within his tier, putting Alyosha above Valeriy due to the former's more universal nature when it comes to team placement.

Tier List Criteria and Notes
Tier List Overview Criteria
As we theorize and test characters, they are not treated as a singular unit but as a core member in their best team comp. In other words, characters should not be judged in isolation. Here you can check the summary of explanations for each tier:

Genshin - SS Rank IconSS Tier (Tier 0)	Characters that stand out as one of the strongest units in their intended team comps, who also have the edge of potentially staying relevant for long periods of time, and are actively in the forefront of supported characters as new ones are released.
Main DPS: The sheer damage potential ensures that their longevity can be maintained, not to mention their great ease of use and comfortability to defeat enemies. They greatly utilize existing and any potential future support units as they are able to be inserted in the best team comps within their respective elements. They are the best utilizers of the current best Sub-DPS and Supports and can dominate endgame with ease.
Sub DPS: Characters who are the best Elemental Applicators and teammates in several top-tier team comps, all while having good damage of their own.
Support: Universal buffer supports whose utility are essential to increase most top-tier team comps' overall damage and comfort, and sustain supports who offer the best talents to prevent your party from ever being wiped out while still having good offensive utility of their own.
S Rank IconS Tier (Tier 0.5)	Characters that reach their peak strength with the recommended amount of investment, especially with acquiring allies that serve to bring them up to par with more competitive options. There's only small room for improvement and they ensure that you can beat any content with ease. These are all the best characters in their respective elements, but not quite as broken as the characters in the SS-Tier.
Main DPS: The starting point of the best characters at the recommended level of investment. They can match up against the best characters in the highest tier but need a little bit more investment in their weapons, constellations, or other equipment.
Sub DPS: Characters with superb damage, or are often the core applicators for some relevant team comps in the game. There may be certain alternatives who are able to work better in a particular team comp.
Support: Offer one of the best buffs or sustain in their own particular niche, but are less accessible if put to other team comps.
A+ Rank IconA+ Tier (Tier 1)	Characters who can only become strong with maximum investment on all aspects (artifacts, weapons, team comps, constellations) to achieve similar performance as the other characters in the upper tiers. This is the cut-off point for the strongest characters in their respective roles.
Main DPS: Requires max investment on both their own and all three of their other teammates to be able to match up against characters in the upper tiers.
Sub DPS: Good Elemental Applicators or damage dealers up until a point, who specifically shine in their own team comps. They are also often used in some niche or specialized team comps.
Support: Offer specialized buffs or sustain, but have certain limitations in their own kits that prevent them from being more universal.
A Rank IconA Tier (Tier 1.5)	Characters with solid performance in their respective roles. They shine in specific team compositions, but not so much on their own.
Main DPS: Characters who require max investment, but may still not be enough to compete against the best DPS in the game due to limitations on their kit, stats, or talent scalings, and get no sufficient help from current meta units.
Sub DPS: Decent Elemental Application and damage on their respective team comps, but are not entirely required on such parties.
Support: Characters who are good at their intended role, but see minimal usage from the top-tier team comps versus the other characters in the higher tiers.
B Rank IconB Tier (Tier 2)	Characters who can do the job for their respective role, but can be replaced quickly due to mere lack of good numbers from their talents or stats, or are stunted by their own cooldowns and energy requirement.
Main DPS: Damage scaling is lower in comparison, but are able to do their job in the right team comp. More often than not, it's better to pick characters who can trigger better reactions.
Sub DPS: Offer some manner of application and damage, but are stunted by their own scalings or rate of application.
Support: Characters who can offer sustain or buffs and are decent at doing it. However, better alternatives exist, which are easier or more efficient to use in the long run.
C Rank IconC Tier (Tier 3)	Characters with limited combat utility. You can use these characters if you are truly dedicated, but there are more convenient options than these.
Main DPS: Damage scaling is low enough to the point where you might have difficulty clearing some content.
Sub DPS: Extremely niche and only offers an advantage over a limited use case and at limited moments.
Support: Characters that have some manner of support, but are outright replaced with mediocre characters that are more accessible or useful in a given team comp.
D Rank IconD Tier (Tier 4)	Characters that are fundamentally limited in almost all areas of their kit. You can use these characters if you don't have anyone else, but we recommend replacing them with a better character for that role.
Main DPS: Damage scaling is very low to be able to keep up, even against elite mobs.
Sub DPS: Offers no real edge in terms of damage and application against characters in all other tiers.
Support: Characters that can offer some manner of support, but has great difficulty in doing so.
Role Breakdown
Role	Function
Main DPS	・The main damage dealer of the party, which is often the hypercarry, the on-field driver, or the trigger for Elemental Reactions.
・Damage dealt is mostly from the character being on-field for an extended period of time, often being the final character in your team's rotations.
Sub DPS	・Providers of off-field damage and utility via Elemental Application. These characters focus on helping the Main DPS trigger reactions by setting up the necessary elements, while also dealing damage of their own.
・Characters with Talents that stay on the field even after being switched out.
・Characters with damaging talents that are meant for quick-swap teams.
Support	・Provides support in the form of buffs, shields, heals, or crowd control via the use of their Elemental Skills, Burst or Passive Talents, and are primarily used off-field.
・Have Talents capable for elemental application, not necessarily for dealing damage.
Tier List by Constellations
Constellation levels listed in the Main Tier List are considered the ideal level for that character's role.
It is important to note that characters are evaluated in comparison with other characters in the current meta, and as such, may affect their rankings in all tier lists.
For example:
• Xingqiu's C6 is considered his optimal constellation and is thus ranked on that power level.
• Hu Tao and Wriothesley C1 open up new playstyles and combat rotations, and so it may be considered as the better starting point before they are assessed on their power level.
On Changes to the Tier List
Changes to the tier list are delicate and periodical, and we understand that any changes made to a tier can be drastic and require explanations. Below, you can find four scenarios within a patch's life cycle and our plan of action when it occurs:

Scenario	Notes
A new character releases: Preliminary Rankings	Expect preliminary rankings for a character when they first release. These will not be their final rankings in the version and will always be subject to change. As we'll need time to test their kit out, we urge our readers to treat the rankings on their first week as tentative. These will now be denoted with aNEW symbol.
A character's banner will soon end: Final Rankings	Around a week before the new character's banner ends, we will be finalizing our thoughts about the character's tier.
While we refer to this as the final ranking, characters released in the future may affect previous characters' overall performances. Those characters will be subjected to change as well.
Meta Shifts	Shifts to the combat system like new mechanics or the release of a powerful support character can shake up our tier list. A concrete example of this was Dendro shaking up the meta when it first released, resulting in characters like Shinobu, Keqing, and Thoma, to rise up in rankings.
Fixes	Feedback that explains in detail why a character may or may not be in the correct tiering can be reconsidered for deliberation.

Exploration Tier List Criteria
Genshin - Exploration SS RankSS Tier	Characters who have Skills or Talents that make either land, air, or sea exploration the easiest, or have talents that are considered invaluable for farming materials, puzzle solving, or ease of survival.
Exploration S RankS Tier	Characters who have talents for land, air, or sea exploration, but are less comparable against the characters in the SS tier.
Exploration A RankA Tier	Characters who have talents that can increase movement speed or reduce stamina consumption. Characters who have talents that help farming overworld materials are also included.
Exploration B RankB Tier	Characters who at least have some utility inherent with their weapon or Element, such as elemental totem activation, or target practice with bows. Talents that help with the game's secondary content like Fishing are also included.
Exploration C RankC Tier	Characters having limited or no utility at all for overworld exploration.
