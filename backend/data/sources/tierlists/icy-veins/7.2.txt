2. Genshin Impact Version 7.2 Tier List Changes and Additions
Below are the most recent changes to our tier list which is the changes in patch 7.2 which focuses on the release of  Mitya and  Valeriy.

Character	Explanation
Mitya	
 Mitya is the new 5-star character arriving in patch 7.2 and is an exceptionally strong and versatile unit, capable of functioning as both an on-field Main DPS and an off-field support, excelling in either role. His main drawback as a Main DPS is that he competes for many of the same premium supports, particularly  Yae Miko and  Odette, that other Stellar DPS characters also rely on. However, in a support role, Mitya can often fill a similar team slot to Yae Miko or even work alongside her, offering significantly greater potential for vertical investment.

Valeriy	
 Valeriy follows the trend of niche 4-star supports who, while potentially very powerful, are limited to a small selection of teams and often rely heavily on their C6 to reach their full potential. His buffs are primarily tailored towards Stellar Conduct teams centred around Electro Main DPS characters, meaning that currently only  Cyno and  Mitya can fully utilise his kit. Additionally, Valeriy has relatively lengthy animations that cause considerable field time, resulting in longer rotations.

3. Tier List Explanations and Ranking Criteria
Our Tier List for Genshin Impact groups characters as either a Main DPS, Sub DPS or a support.

Main DPS: A character who spends the majority of their time on-field in the team to deal massive damage.
Sub DPS: Characters that have good elemental application to create Elemental Reactions for the team or deal high amounts of damage while off-field.
Support: Healers, Shielders and buffers. These characters may also deal some damage while off-field but their value lies in the supportive utility they give the party.
Characters are then ranked in their respective role based on D to S+ rankings based on their damage, ease of play, elemental application, buffs they provide to the team and how widely used they are.

Characters in the same tier are simply in alphabetical order and generally speaking it is pointless to compare characters in the same tier due to them rarely overlapping directly in roles and having different elements that allow them to be more or less suitable based on the fight.
